About Kural 594

Thirukkural · Book of Wealth (பொருட்பால்) · Chapter 60 — ஊக்கமுடைமை (Possession of Zeal)

ஆக்கம் அதர்வினாய்ச் செல்லும் அசைவிலா
ஊக்க முடையா னுழை.

Aakkam adharvinaaych chellum asaivilaa / Ookka mudaiyaan uzhai.

Meaning: Prosperity asks the way and goes to the one who has unshakable zeal. Wealth does not wait to be chased by the lazy — it seeks out the person whose determination never wavers.

Thiruvalluvar

The Tamil poet-sage credited with the Thirukkural, a classic of ethics and practical wisdom composed in short two-line couplets called kurals. Its teachings are universal, so it is read and quoted far beyond Tamil Nadu.

The Thirukkural

1,330 couplets in 133 chapters of ten kurals each, divided into three books: Aram (virtue, 38 chapters), Porul (wealth and governance, 70 chapters) and Inbam (love, 25 chapters).

Life lessons

🔥 Consistency beats talent

Small daily effort outlasts bursts of motivation.

🌱 Results lag effort

The well looked empty for days; the water was always coming.

🚪 Opportunity seeks the ready

Wealth passed the sleeper and found the digger.

🛡 Guard your spirit

Doubt and sloth are the real obstacles — not the soil.
